    What's included

    What each part helps you do

    Three website templates

    Single-page websites with an intro, what you offer, an about section, how it works, a short FAQ and a WhatsApp contact button. Each one is a single file that adapts to phone and desktop screens.

    Customization and publishing guide

    Shows how to make a safe copy, where to change text and colors, how to set up the contact button, how to check the page in your browser and how to publish it on a static hosting service. Includes fixes for common problems.

    AI prompts

    Ready-to-adapt requests for customizing content, changing the color palette, setting up contact on WhatsApp or switching it to email, asking for an independent review and describing a bug so the AI can suggest a small fix.

    Pre-publish checklist

    A list to confirm before going live: real information only, working links, no horizontal scrolling on phones, keyboard navigation, no passwords or keys in the code, and HTTPS after publishing.

    Who it's for

    Is this the right fit?

    A good fit if you

    • Want a simple page that presents your small business or your work.
    • Are willing to edit a file, try changes and check the result yourself.
    • Want to learn the basics needed to review what an AI assistant changes.
    • Prefer to start from an organized structure instead of a blank page.

    Not the right fit if you need

    • An online store, payments or a shopping cart.
    • User accounts, logins, a database or an admin panel.
    • A booking or management system.
    • Someone to customize or publish the site for you.

    Those features require a separate project, with its own infrastructure and testing.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Do I need to know how to code?

    You don't need prior coding experience to start, but you will edit HTML files in a text editor. The guide shows where to change text, colors and contact details. Expect to learn a few basics along the way so you can check that everything works.

    Can I use a template for my business?

    Yes. You can adapt the templates and use the result on personal or commercial websites. You can't resell or redistribute the original package as your own product. Any images, logos or text you add must be yours or used with permission.

    Can I customize it using Claude or another AI?

    Yes. The prompts are written for general-purpose AI assistants, such as Claude or similar tools. AI subscriptions are not included. AI can make mistakes, so the guide recommends small changes and checking the result after each one.

    Do visitors contact me through WhatsApp?

    The templates come with a WhatsApp button that opens a chat with your business number. If you prefer email, the prompts include a request to switch the button to an email link. The templates don't include contact forms.

    Are a domain and hosting included?

    No. The guide explains how to publish on a hosting service that accepts static websites. Hosting services and domains have their own terms and may have costs. Check them before signing up.

    Does the package include an online store or an admin panel?

    No. The templates are presentation pages. They don't include a store, payments, logins, a database, a management system or forms that collect data.

    Are there videos?

    No. This version doesn't include video lessons. The package is made of files: templates, a written guide, prompts and a checklist.
